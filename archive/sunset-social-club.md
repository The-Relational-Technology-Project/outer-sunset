---
title: Sunset Social Club, what it is and how to join
---

The Sunset Social Club is a nonprofit neighborhood club run by people who live in the Sunset. Its vision: "a Sunset where everyone knows someone on every block." It's for anyone who lives in the Sunset or is a friend of the Sunset. It's free.

## What the club does

It organizes gatherings that help neighbors get to know each other and have a hand in shaping the neighborhood. The hope is that over time it becomes a steady place where neighbors know one another by name, share resources, support local businesses, organize projects, and celebrate together.

Gatherings so far, in 2026:

- July 22: kick-off welcome and pizza party
- August 26: neighborhood potluck
- September 30: creative play workshop, led by a neighbor
- October 9: the first Sunset neighborhood build-a-thon
- November 18: Backyard Tea + Tai Chi, 5:30 to 7:30pm near 22nd Ave and Quintara, all ages, register on the club's calendar

Most gatherings so far were at 4114 Judah St, the Sunset Village Music Hall. Others are in neighbors' yards and around the neighborhood.

The club also organizes the Sunset Wave: neighbor gatherings on every block on Saturday, March 20, 2027. See https://thesunsetwave.org.

## How to join

Go to https://sunsetsocialclub.org/join. It asks for an email; first name and cross streets are optional. Members get occasional email updates about gatherings and ways to get involved. As of November 2026 the club had about 350 members.

## What's coming up

The club's calendar is at https://luma.com/sunsetsocialclub. RSVP there. Bring a friend or come alone.

## Ideas

Anyone can share an idea for the club on the public board on its home page. Ideas neighbors have posted: communal batch cooking, an arts and crafts club, a mahjong night, a bao-stuffing night, a gear swap for surfboards and camping gear, a book and toy exchange, a junk journal workshop. The tea and tai chi gathering started as an idea on that board.

## To host or sponsor a gathering

Write to the club through https://sunsetsocialclub.org/contact or at oursunsetsocialclub@gmail.com.

## Who runs it

Neighbors. Sandi Lam Harder and Josh Nesbit founded it and are its stewards. Reach them at oursunsetsocialclub@gmail.com. The club is on Instagram at @oursunsetsocialclub. The site is in English and Chinese.

## Privacy and care

The club doesn't sell or share members' information and doesn't use tracking cookies. Members can delete their account at any time. If a misunderstanding or conflict comes up, the club's stewards will help neighbors talk it through.

# Archive

History and field notes for the Outer Sunset, gathered from public sources. Knowy answers from these, a section at a time, and then points to a person who knows more.

## How a note is written

- One subject per `##` section, 80 to 170 words, readable on its own.
- Every section ends with a `Sources:` line linking the pages it came from.
- Years, names, cross streets, species. The why, not only the what.
- A last section, "Who knows more", naming groups a neighbor can ask.
- `history-*.md` for the eras of the place, `ecology-*.md` for its more-than-human neighbors.

These are a place to start, not the last word. They were gathered in November 2026 and will have mistakes. A neighbor's correction (`corrections/`) or own account (`knowledge/`) wins.

## Public archives worth an afternoon

- [OpenSFHistory](https://opensfhistory.org/) by the Western Neighborhoods Project: photographs of the Sunset back to the dunes and the Carville houses. Search "Sunset District" or a street name.
- [SFPL San Francisco History Center](https://sfpl.org/locations/main-library/san-francisco-history-center) and the [SFPL photo collection](https://sfpl.org/locations/main-library/san-francisco-history-center/san-francisco-historical-photograph).
- [Western Neighborhoods Project](https://www.outsidelands.org/): the Outside Lands podcast and articles, street by street.

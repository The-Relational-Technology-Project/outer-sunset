title: The Sunset Wave, how to host or join a gathering
---

How hosting and joining work for the Sunset Wave, the day of neighbor gatherings across the Sunset on Saturday, March 20, 2027. From the questions and answers at https://thesunsetwave.org, read November 9, 2026.

## To host

Go to https://thesunsetwave.org and tap "Host on your block". The form asks for your name, an email or phone, and your stretch (for example, "44th Ave between Judah and Kirkham"). You can add co-hosts, say your block could use some funds, and say whether your spot is sheltered from rain.

Then pick a spot on your block, invite your neighbors, and show up on March 20 with what you need. The suggested time is 11am to 1pm, but how long you host and what you do is up to you.

Ideas from the site: card games in the garage, a potluck out front, coffee and donuts, chalk art and music, tea on the steps, a kids' bike parade.

## What hosts get

A hosting kit, a party for hosts, a network of other hosts, planning help, flyers by local artists, a little money if you need it, and day-of support.

## Permits

Most gatherings on a sidewalk, in a driveway, or in a garage won't need one. If you want to close the street, the organizers can help you apply for a permit.

## Someone is already hosting on my block

You can ask the organizers to send them a note. You could offer to co-host, or host somewhere nearby. More than one host on a block is fine.

## Money

If your block could use support, say so when you sign up; the organizers will help where they can. If you can help another block, check "I can give a little" on the form, or donate to the Sunset Gatherings Fund. Donations are tax-deductible.

## Rain or fog

It goes ahead. Garages and warm food and drinks will do well. Hosts will get a rainy-day plan and ideas.

## To join without hosting

Find your block on the map at https://thesunsetwave.org/#map. A wave on a block means a neighbor there has raised a hand. On the day, walk outside.

## Privacy

Host names and contact details are never shown on the map. If you send a note to a host, the stewards read it first and then pass it along. Nobody, including Knowy, can tell you who is hosting on a given block; the way to reach a host is a note through the site.

## Languages

The site is in English, Mandarin, Cantonese, and Spanish.

## If something goes wrong

Sunset Social Club stewards will help neighbors talk it through. Write to oursunsetsocialclub@gmail.com.

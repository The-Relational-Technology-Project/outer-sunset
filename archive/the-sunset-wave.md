---
title: The Sunset Wave, what it is and who's behind it
---

The Sunset Wave is a day of neighbor gatherings on blocks all across the Sunset: Saturday, March 20, 2027, the spring equinox. The aim is about 400 gatherings, one on every block, each hosted by a neighbor who lives there. The site puts it as "Gatherings on every block. On the same day."

## Who's behind it

The Sunset Social Club organizes it. Sandi Lam Harder and Josh Nesbit, two Sunset neighbors who founded the club, are the stewards of the Wave. Reach them at oursunsetsocialclub@gmail.com or through the form at https://thesunsetwave.org/contact.

The Sunset Social Club is free and open to anyone who wants to know their neighbors. As of November 2026 it had about 350 members, and 100 people came to its first event. It meets at the Sunset Village Music Hall, a former church on Judah. The club's site is https://sunsetsocialclub.org.

## Why

The organizers want more neighbors to meet each other, including neighbors who are different ages, have lived here different lengths of time, or disagree about neighborhood politics. The name is a nod to the ocean and to a friendly wave hello. They picked the equinox because spring is a fresh start, because that date has often been sunny, and because it left five months to find hosts. They hope it becomes a yearly tradition.

## Where

Across the Sunset, from Lincoln to south of Sloat, roughly west of 19th Avenue. The numbered avenues are the first focus; the named streets are welcome too. That's wider than the Outer Sunset alone. The map at https://thesunsetwave.org/#map shows which blocks have a host and which are open.

## Where it stands

On November 9, 2026, the site showed 88 neighbors had raised a hand to host. The map has the current count.

## Money and sponsors

Donations go through the Sunset Gatherings Fund on Open Collective, hosted by the Relational Tech Project, and are tax-deductible: https://opencollective.com/relational-tech-project/projects/sunset-gatherings-fund. The Workday Foundation gave $5,000. The organizers hope to raise enough to give a little money to each host who needs it. Local businesses can sponsor a gathering or get involved: https://thesunsetwave.org/contact.

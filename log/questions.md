# Question log

Aggregated by topic and ISO week. No names, no handles, no message text. One row per exchange: the topic the question fell under, whether the answer ended by pointing to a person or group, whether the agent said "I don't know", and whether the neighbor said a connection happened because of Knowy (they went, met someone, joined). Connections are only counted when a neighbor volunteers it; Knowy never asks.

This is the quiet prize: a live map of what neighbors are looking for and can't find. Stewards read it to see unmet needs. The agent appends to it by PR once a week.

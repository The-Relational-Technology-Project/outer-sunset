Neighbor gatherings on blocks all across the Sunset on the same day, Saturday, March 20, 2027, the spring equinox. The aim is 400 of them. Each one is hosted by a neighbor on their own block: a driveway, a garage, a parklet, a corner. Coffee and donuts, a potluck, card games, chalk and music, a kids' bike parade, whatever the block wants.

11am to 1pm is the suggested time; each host picks their own hours. Free. To host, raise your hand at thesunsetwave.org. To join, find your block on the map there. Questions: oursunsetsocialclub@gmail.com.

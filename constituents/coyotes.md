# The coyotes

Coyotes live in Golden Gate Park, the Presidio, and the dunes, and they walk the avenues at dusk and dawn. They were here before the houses. They are not a problem to solve; they are neighbors to live alongside.

## What the agent should know

- Recent sightings come from iNaturalist observations in the Outer Sunset bounding box. The agent can say when one was last reported and where, with the observation link.
- Pupping season is roughly April through August, when coyotes are more protective and more visible.
- Hazing (standing tall, making noise, not running) is the advice from SF Animal Care and Control for a coyote that gets too close. Keep dogs leashed, keep cats in at night, don't leave food out.

## What it needs neighbors to know

- Seeing a coyote is normal here and not an emergency. Report an aggressive one to SF Animal Care and Control, 415-554-6364.
- Never feed them. A fed coyote is a dead coyote.

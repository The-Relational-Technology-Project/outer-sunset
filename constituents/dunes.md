# The dunes

Two kinds of dunes live here. The beach dunes along Ocean Beach are restored habitat managed by the National Park Service, planted with native dune grasses that hold the sand. Sunset Dunes is the park along the old Great Highway, opened in 2025 and run by SF Rec and Park, where the pavement is slowly becoming paths, art, and planted dune.

## What the agent should know

- Dune plants are fragile; the fenced areas on the beach side are closed to walking so the sand stays put.
- iNaturalist observations in the Outer Sunset bounding box give a live sense of what's growing and moving through: dune tansy, beach strawberry, coyote brush, sand verbena.
- NPS alerts for Golden Gate cover closures and construction that touch the beach side.

## What it needs neighbors to know

- Stay on the paths through the dunes. The signs are not decorative.
- An event in Sunset Dunes needs a permit from Rec and Park. The agent should say so when a neighbor proposes one, and name Rec and Park as the place to ask.

## Never

Speak as the dunes.

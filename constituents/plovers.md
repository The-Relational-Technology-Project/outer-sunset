# The plovers

Western snowy plovers are small, sand-colored shorebirds listed as threatened. They don't nest at Ocean Beach, but they winter here, resting on the upper beach from roughly July through May. A resting plover that gets flushed by a dog or a runner burns energy it needs to survive the winter.

## What the agent should know

- The protection season runs July 1 through May 15 each year. During it, dogs must be on a six-foot leash in the Snowy Plover Protection Area (the southern stretch of Ocean Beach, marked on the NPS map). Outside the season, voice control is allowed in that area. Source: NPS Ocean Beach page.
- The agent computes "in season" or "out of season" from today's date and the dates above. It does not guess beyond that.
- Recent plover sightings show up in iNaturalist within the Outer Sunset bounding box.

## What it needs neighbors to know

- If a neighbor proposes a beach event, a run, or anything with dogs between July and mid-May, the agent should bring up the plover season and the leash rule unprompted, with the NPS source.
- Walk the wet sand near the water, not the upper beach, when the plovers are resting.

## Never

Speak as the plovers. "The plovers are wintering here through May 15 (NPS)" is fine. "The plovers ask you to..." is not.
